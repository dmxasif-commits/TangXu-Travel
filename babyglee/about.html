About us

Because we love them

Babyglee makes comfortable, safe and affordable sneakers for children aged 1 to 10. Our shoes are designed in-house and made in Bangladesh, with the sizes, materials and details that growing feet actually need.

Our story

Babyglee was founded in 2016 on a simple belief: kids deserve well-made shoes at honest prices. Our tagline says it best: Because we love them! Every design starts with comfort and foot development, then adds the colours and details kids love.

What goes into every pair

  • Multi-density sole for joint protection
  • Memory-foam insole for all-day comfort
  • Wide toe box designed with foot development in mind
  • Fast-lock velcro strap so children can put shoes on alone
  • Anti-slip rubber tread for playgrounds, courts and skate parks

Quality & safety

We follow chemical limits for REACH, phthalates and azo dyes, and test for flex resistance, outsole abrasion and strap tensile strength. Every batch is inspected before packing, and batch testing is available on request.

Design & production
