Privacy policy

Last updated: 2025

This policy explains how Babyglee ("we", "us") collects and uses your information when you visit babygleebd.com or place an order.

Information we collect

  • Order details: your name, mobile number, delivery address, chosen product, size and quantity.
  • Messages: anything you send us by WhatsApp, phone or email.
  • Usage data: basic, anonymous information about how the site is used (pages visited, device type), which may be collected through cookies and analytics or advertising tools such as Meta Pixel.

How we use it

  • To confirm, deliver and support your order, including calling or messaging you about size and delivery.
  • To handle exchanges and customer service requests.
  • To understand how our site and ads perform and to improve them.

Sharing

We share your name, phone number and address only with courier and delivery partners so your parcel can reach you. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies & advertising

We may use cookies and similar technologies, including the Meta Pixel, to measure ad performance and show relevant ads. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

Data retention & security

We keep order information for as long as needed to fulfil your order and meet legal and accounting requirements. We take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by contacting us. We will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

Children

Our products are for children, but our website is intended for parents and guardians. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
