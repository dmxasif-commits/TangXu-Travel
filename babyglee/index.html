Our story

Because we love them

FAQ

Quick answers

How does delivery work?

We deliver to your home across the areas we serve. Our team calls or messages you to confirm your size and address before shipping.

Can I pay on delivery?

Yes. Cash on Delivery is available on every order.

What if the size doesn't fit?

You can exchange within 72 hours of delivery. Delivery and return charges apply. See our Shipping & Returns page.

Which sizes do you have?

The Bounce series is available in EU sizes 21 to 36, suitable for roughly ages 1 to 10.

Are the shoes safe for kids?

They are made from microfiber synthetic leather and breathable mesh, with chemical limits for REACH, phthalates and azo dyes.
