# Treatment: "ইঞ্জিনিয়ারিং ডায়েরি — পর্ব ১: বুর্জ খলিফা" — Engineering Briefing Board

Scope of this round: a **~60 s test** (script paragraphs 1–3), 1920×1080 (16:9), 24 fps, Bangla voice-over (Microsoft bn-BD male, Pradeep), Bangla subtitles. Marker sketches only (no photographs). Facts listed in FACTS.md with sources, checked before delivery.

## Three structures
1. **One measurement that keeps growing**: a dimension line for 828 m grows until it breaks the board; each section is a new bigger frame. Fits "how tall", but the script's question is *how does it stand*, not *how tall*.
2. **A checklist stamped item by item**: load, wind, materials, each stamped as the camera moves down the wall. Too list-like for a story told in three movements.
3. **Ground first, then sky (chosen)**: open on the number 828 m and the question; the camera then drops *below* the ground line (the script's own turn: "the story starts underground"), draws piles and the raft foundation, then rises along a leader line to the tower. The ending of this test is the cliffhanger the script already has: "foundation done is not the end" → wind arrow arrives, the tower tilts a degree and the camera holds. Chosen because the script's strongest image is the inversion (the answer is under the ground).

## Logline and arc
How does a tower 828 m tall stand against the wind? The answer starts underground.
- Setup 0–14 s: the number 828 m, the tower sketch, the question in red.
- Turn 14–26 s: "but it starts under the ground" — camera rides a leader line down below the ground line.
- Foundation 26–52 s: excavation (Jan 2004), piles drawn one by one, the raft slab laid on top, load arrows travelling down through piles into the ground.
- Hook 52–60 s: wind arrow arrives from the left, the tower wavers; red text: "ভিত হলেই শেষ নয়".
